Privacy Policy

Your mail stays between you and your mail provider.

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Kurir is made by Carl-Fredrik Arvidson in Sweden (carl-fredrik@arvidson.io). This policy covers the Kurir apps for iPhone and Mac and Kurir Push.

In short: there is no Kurir account and no Kurir server that holds your mail. The apps contain no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The developer never receives your mail, your password or your contacts.

Your mail and your password

Kurir connects straight from your device to your mail provider over IMAP and SMTP, and to the calendars you add over CalDAV or as .ics links. Your app password is stored in your device's keychain. Mail is kept on your device so you can read it offline.

Your settings

Your screening choices, rules, snoozes, contacts, groups, collections and other settings are kept in hidden folders in your own mailbox, so your iPhone and Mac agree without a server in between. Kurir also saves a daily backup of them there. Your mail provider stores these folders like the rest of your mail. Turn on encryption and they are sealed with a passphrase only you know.

Notifications

When Kurir Push runs, on your Mac or on a server you install it on, new-mail notifications go through a relay the developer runs at kurir-notify.arvidson.io, hosted by Vercel, on their way to Apple's Push Notification service. Each one carries the sender's name or address, the subject, an identifier for the thread, a link into the app, your unread count and your device's push token. The relay passes it on and stores nothing, and it does not log what a notification says. The hosting provider may keep standard request logs, such as the IP address and time, for a short while.

Without Kurir Push, your iPhone checks for new mail itself and shows notifications without the relay.

AI features

AI features are off until you connect your own Claude Code subscription in Settings > AI model. Then AI Rules send each mail they judge (your rule, the sender, the subject, the date and the text) to Anthropic, and Generate draft sends the mail you answer and recent mail with that sender when you tap it. This happens under your Anthropic account and Anthropic's terms. If you run Kurir Push yourself, you can set it up with another provider instead, and the same mail goes to that provider. None of it reaches the developer.

AI assistants (MCP)

If you turn on MCP on your Mac or run Kurir Push, assistants you give a token can read, file and draft your mail. The server runs on your own Mac or server, and you can revoke a token at any time.

Images in mail

Remote images in a mail load from the sender's servers only as your setting allows. Images that report when you open a mail are blocked.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your name or payment details.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple passes crash reports and similar diagnostics to the developer. They contain no mail.

Your rights

The only personal data the developer handles is what passes through the notification relay, and for that the developer is responsible under the GDPR. Your mail is a matter between you and your mail provider. For questions or requests, write to carl-fredrik@arvidson.io. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
